These are unpublished draft articles for the future "Marbles" knowledge hub on marblespins.com, written for the owner's review. They are not imported or built by the site. Each file has front matter (title, description, section, slug, type, status, sources) and a markdown body with numbered citations matching the sources list; anything marked [REVIEW: ...] still needs a person to check it. To publish one, review it and resolve every [REVIEW] mark, set `status: published`, and move it to `client/src/hub/articles/`.

26 Oct 2026: the first five (marble types, how to play, Ringer, history, how glass marbles are made) were reviewed, their [REVIEW] marks resolved, and moved to `client/src/hub/articles/`. Their pictures are made by `client/scripts/hub-art/` (sources and alt texts in `images/<article>/pictures.json`); the site's copies are in `client/public/hub-img/<article>/` and `client/public/share/marbles/`, indexed in `client/src/hub/pictures.json`.
