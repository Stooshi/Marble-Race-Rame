# 6. Decisions log

*Last updated 14 Oct 2026.* Key product and technical decisions, newest first, each with its reason. Decisions are Moses's unless marked "(technical)". Add new ones at the top. Don't rewrite old entries: add a new entry that replaces them and say so.

| Date | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 2026-10-14 | **Canyon Series:** 10 tracks through real canyons, a family of their own (`canyon`), added **switched off**. Each canyon is built as a coaster that runs on the canyon floor and climbs out over the rock on supports (timber trestles on Copper and Hells); every track has its own layout, start and signature. | Moses's series plan. Its own family keeps its openings and starts varied among themselves; building on the coaster keeps every race rule and check unchanged. |
| 2026-10-14 | **Canyons are shaped into the land itself** (technical): floor, stepped walls and plateau come from the track's own line, drawn finer along the walls (coarser on phones), and give way to the ordinary hillside where the track passes over itself (a spiral's turns). The land beside any stretch never rises over it. | Walls follow every bend with no gaps, inside a tight bend they meet in a butte, nothing can stand in the track, and phones stay within the drawing budget. |
| 2026-10-14 | **The follow camera never eases up into a coaster's station canopy** (technical): easing down to its spot under the canopy, it could lag inside the front beam for a frame or two (seen on Copper Spins). Looks only. | The camera rule: nothing drawn within 0.5 m of the camera. |
| 2026-10-14 | **No attraction names in canyon scenery** (a "glass skywalk", not a brand); real canyon and river names in track names and descriptions only. | Trademarks. |
| 2026-10-14 | **Everyone races at the same level for launch** (skill 50 for every player); all texts say so. A growth rule is to be revisited after launch with real data. | Honest, simple and fair from day one. Growth needs real play data to tune without making the game feel unfair. |
| 2026-10-14 | **Email through Brevo** (EU). Password reset is built now and turns on when `BREVO_API_KEY` is set on Railway; news emails will use the same provider. | An EU provider keeps personal data in the EU. Building it behind a setting means it works the moment the key is added. |
| 2026-10-14 | **Coin entry fees switched off completely** (server and screens). Every race is free to enter. | Coins can be bought, so paying coins to enter a race and winning coins by place would look like betting. That means "simulated gambling" in the app stores and a 13+ rating or higher. |
| 2026-10-14 | **Google Play target audience 13+** until parental approval for under-13s is built. No Apple "Made for Kids". The server refuses under-13 sign-ups, as the sign-up page does (technical). | Keeps the app outside the Families policy while the under-13 rules are a placeholder. |
| 2026-10-14 | **Keep iPad support** in the iPhone app. | The game already works well on tablets. Cost: iPad screenshots for the store. |
| 2026-10-14 | **No analytics** (and the privacy policy says so). | No analytics are collected, so the policy must not claim it. Simpler store privacy forms. |
| 2026-10-14 | **App id `com.marblespins.app`** for both apps. Test builds on iPhone use `.dev`. | Permanent store identity. Test builds mustn't take the real id under a free Apple account. |
| 2026-10-14 | **Apps wrap the website with Capacitor**: icons, splash screen, offline screen, 60 fps cap on phones. | One codebase for web, iPhone and Android, with native touches where stores expect them. |
| 2026-10-14 | **Fonts served with the site**, not by Google Fonts (technical). | Faster, private (no visitor IPs to Google under GDPR), and works offline in the apps. |
| 2026-10-14 | **marblespins.com and www both work.** Canonical links point at marblespins.com; Stripe returns the player to the address they bought from. | Players stay logged in, because each address has its own login storage. Search engines see one site. |
| 2026-10-14 | **Player self-service:** download my data, and delete my account. Deletion anonymises the account, keeps payment records, and asks for confirmation. | GDPR, and Apple's and Google's rule that apps with accounts must offer deletion. Results stay so other players' places never change. Bookkeeping law requires 7 years of payment records. |
| 2026-10-14 | **News by email** is a setting saved with its date. Signed unsubscribe link. **Never offered under 13.** | Consent must be provable and easy to withdraw. Children's privacy. |
| 2026-10-14 | **Stripe on the web.** Coins only after Stripe's signed webhook, never twice. Order numbers. Prices in local currency with VAT included (Adaptive Pricing + Stripe Tax). Consent to immediate delivery and loss of the 14-day withdrawal right. Refunds remove coins. **Test mode until Moses says go** (`STRIPE_ALLOW_LIVE`). | Correct, provable payments under EU consumer law. No free coins from fake or repeated payment messages. No accidental live charges. |
| 2026-10-14 | **Back office** on Vercel under `/admin`: hidden, noindex, all data through the server. The server checks admin rights on every request. 2-step login, 30-minute timeout, a reason and audit-log entry for every action. | Support needs real tools, and hiding pages is not security. |
| 2026-10-14 | **IP addresses only for security and fraud:** the full IP at most 90 days, then only the country. | GDPR data minimisation. Enough to catch coin farming and payment fraud. |
| 2026-10-14 | **Friend rooms:** private races by code. Same coin pay as any race, counted in the daily limit. No chat. | Play with friends without opening a way to farm coins. No chat keeps rooms safe for young players. |
| 2026-10-14 | **Music and sound:** 4 tracks shuffled, always mixed under the race sounds. Separate music and effects volumes, plus mute, remembered per player. Nothing before the first tap. Small files. Race sound effects beyond the 3 named sounds: not now. | Atmosphere without annoying players or slowing race starts. Browsers require a tap before sound. |
| 2026-10-14 | **All 3D marble designs approved** for the shop. The **shop picture is the master** for every marble's colours, and 3D is painted from the same data. | What players buy must look the same in the race. |
| 2026-10-13 | **Coins only:** one currency. Credits removed; existing credits converted at 1 = 1,000 coins, and all coin amounts ×10. Earned and bought coins counted apart; **scoreboards count earned coins only**. | Simpler for players and parents. The scoreboard top is won on the track, not bought. (Replaces the 10 Oct coins-plus-credits plan.) |
| 2026-10-13 | **Coin packs:** $5 = 5,000, $10 = 10,500, $25 = 27,500, $50 = 57,500. | Bonus coins on bigger packs. |
| 2026-10-13 | **Collection 1, Schoolyard Classics:** 10 designs × 5 shades with **Marble IDs** (e.g. SC105). The first **500 copies of every marble are a numbered First Edition** with a certificate; after that it stays on sale unnumbered. One copy per player per marble. | Collectability and a reason to buy early, without making marbles scarce forever. Numbers are handed out under a database lock, so two players never share one. |
| 2026-10-13 | **No loot boxes.** Every purchase is a chosen item; bags let the buyer pick the shade. | Fair to players, store-friendly and legally safer (several countries regulate paid random items). |
| 2026-10-13 | **Free starter set:** every player keeps 4 plain one-colour marbles. No early-player badge. | Everyone can race at once. |
| 2026-10-13 | **Showcase marbles:** in beginner races (everyone under 20 races), computer players wear collection marbles, with a "CPU" tag. | Shows new players what exists. Looks never affect results, and the report checks that every rarity wins its fair share. |
| 2026-10-13 | **Apps with Capacitor** around the same web client. One payment interface: Stripe on the web, Apple/Google in-app purchases in the apps, accepting the stores' cut. | No second codebase. Store rules require store billing for digital items. |
| 2026-10-13 | **Launch under Moses's current Swedish company.** Ownership may move to a new company later. | Speed to launch. |
| 2026-10-13 | **The server is deployed by hand** (ZIP + `railway up`); the website deploys automatically from `Main`. | Control over when the database and live races change. |
| 2026-10-12 | **Skill belongs to the player, not the marble.** Marbles are looks only; coins were refunded for marbles bought for their strength. | **Looks never buy speed**, the core fairness promise. |
| 2026-10-12 | **Name Marble Spins**, domain marblespins.com. Logo: "marblespins" wordmark with a spinning marble as the i-dot, on navy. | .com available, no Swedish words. |
| 2026-10-11 | **Finish ceremony:** a compact gold, silver and bronze banner as the top 3 finish; full confetti only once about 70% of the field is home, at most about 8 s after the winner. | Celebrate without hiding the race. |
| 2026-10-10 | **Coins are the reward system**, not a separate points system. Trading marbles is planned for later. | Players understand coins. |
| 2026-10-10 | **Track variety:** new tracks use varied openings and layouts, with a sameness check. Existing tracks stay as they are for now. | 50 tracks must feel different. |
| 2026-10-09 | **Build a reusable track kit first**, then tracks. **Launch with about 50 tracks**: mirrors count towards the 50 and keep their own records, lighting variants don't, and at least 35 must be truly unique. | A handful of tracks gives only 10–20 minutes of play. The kit makes each new track mostly design work. |
| 2026-10-09 | **iPhone and Android launch together with the web.** Social clips of races posted automatically. Organic growth first. | One launch moment. |
| 2026-10-08 | **Track design rules:** long flowing bends, at most one sharp bend per track; tracks sit in the ground like a trench, raised only where they drop away or cross. | The look and feel Moses wants, and closer racing. |
| 2026-10-07 | **Business model:** free to play, unlimited races, in-app purchases (cosmetics, limited editions) from day one; subscriptions and a season pass later. | Growth first, with revenue from day one. |
| (from the start) | **A race is decided once, on the server, from a seed; results never change** (technical). Classic races are re-simulated from the seed; physics races are stored as replays; race fingerprints guard both. | Trust: replays always match, nobody can change a result, and code changes can't rewrite history. |
| (from the start) | **One server instance** (technical). | Race timers live in memory. Scaling out needs the Redis adapter and race ownership (see the root README). |
| (from the start) | **Railway free plan** for now; Pro later. | Cost until there is traffic. |
